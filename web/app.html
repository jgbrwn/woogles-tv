Woogles-TV

📺 Android TV / Google TV app

A small full-screen app for Google TV boxes (like the Onn 4K). It opens the board, keeps the screen on, and works with the remote.

Download woogles-tv.apk

Install it on the TV

  1. Turn on Developer options: Settings → System → About → press OK on Android TV OS build 7 times.
  2. Install Downloader (by AFTVnews) from the Play Store.
  3. Settings → Apps → Security & restrictions → Unknown sources → turn on Downloader.
  4. Open Downloader and enter , then press Install.
  5. Open Woogles-TV from your apps. Phones scan the QR code to join.

Remote: OK opens options (theme, sound, new room). Long-press OK or Menu for app settings (server URL). Back twice exits.

No app? Use any browser

Any TV or computer browser works: open full-screen.
